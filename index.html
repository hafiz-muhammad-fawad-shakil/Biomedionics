🔒 Privacy Policy

What We Collect

  • Order information: Name, email, phone, address.
  • Payment information: Payment method type (we do not store card numbers).
  • Device data: Force measurements from Isometri Muscle Meter, tied to Device ID (MAC address).
  • Usage data: Basic anonymous usage patterns.

Device Data & Firebase

When you connect your Isometri Muscle Meter, real-time force data is transmitted to Google Firebase and stored in our Realtime Database. Data is associated with your Device ID only. You can disconnect anytime to stop transmission. Historical data stored up to 30 days.
